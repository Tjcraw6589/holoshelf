The service

Holoshelf is a collection tracker and price guide for physical collectibles. It shows market values gathered from third-party sources and lets you record what you own. Values are informational estimates based on third-party data and our own stored history; they are not appraisals, offers to buy, or financial advice, and they can be wrong or out of date. Always verify a value before relying on it. Live eBay listings shown on item pages are sellers' asking prices on eBay, not values from Holoshelf; purchases happen on eBay under eBay's terms, and we may earn a commission from them.

Using Holoshelf with or without an account

An account is optional. Without one, your collection is stored only on your device; if you remove the app or lose the device, that collection is gone, and we cannot recover it. Price alerts, problem reports and photos of your copies need an account.

With an account you sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, with Sign in with Apple, or with Google. You are responsible for keeping access to that mailbox or provider account. Your collection, alerts and settings sync between the devices you sign in on; changes made offline are sent when the device is back online, and when the same item was changed on two devices the most recent change is kept. When you sign in on a device that already has a collection, we ask before adding it to your account.

You may export your data or delete your account at any time from the app (Settings or Account). Deleting removes the account and its data from our servers permanently; it does not cancel a subscription, which is billed and cancelled through your app store account.

Reports, corrections and your photos

You can report a value, picture or detail you believe is wrong. Each report is checked against the item's source and may lead to a correction after review; we may decline any report. Do not file false or abusive reports. Values marked "Holoshelf correction" were set after a report and give way to the source again after a while. Items without licensed artwork show a designed cover, not the real one.

Photos you add of your own items must be yours to share. You keep their rights and give us permission to store them and show them back to you in the app.
